# Venus Pair Whitepaper

## Vision

Attention on Solana moves in hours, not weeks. Venus Pair wants to be the calm point in that orbit: a recognisable asset with a clear identity, and a public board that shows where the volume is going without dressing it up.

## Mission

- Keep a live, honest view of the heaviest boosted Solana tokens.
- Make every address one click from the clipboard, and every claim easy to verify elsewhere.
- Treat the community as readers first and customers never.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Orbit

Launch the site, publish the contract address, and open the official channels.

### Phase 2 — Signal

Widen the board: more sorting views, per-token detail, and clearer risk markers.

### Phase 3 — Gravity

Community tooling around the board, from watchlists to shareable snapshots.

### Phase 4 — Horizon

Open the code, document the data sources, and invite outside contributors.
